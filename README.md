# Helldivers Arcade

Arcade game modes for HELLDIVERS (2015), for the [Obsidian](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianLauncher) mod manager. The first mode is Super Earth Survivors.

## Super Earth Survivors

A mode inspired by Vampire Survivors. The squad drops on an open map and holds out against waves that grow stronger over time. A second faction joins at 6 minutes and the third at 12, with elites, bosses and bombardments along the way.

Kills drop research samples. Each level up freezes the fight, refills health and lets every player pick one of three upgrade cards; every third level adds a stratagem. Some upgrades come in pairs that can evolve into a stronger one. The shuttle can be called after 15 minutes, and a summary of the run shows at the end.

## How to use

Open the galaxy map and pick the **ARCADE** tab, beside Proving Grounds. Choose the faction, the difficulty and the biome, then Launch. The next drop plays the same mission again until a campaign mission is picked.

Arcade missions leave the Galactic Campaign as it was.

## Playing with others

Until the host picks an arcade mission, the mod runs as a client mod and anyone can join. An arcade mission needs every player in the lobby to run Helldivers Arcade at the same version.
